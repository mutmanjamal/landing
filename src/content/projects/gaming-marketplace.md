## Problem

A client needed a site to sell digital game cards online. They didn't want a framework or ongoing hosting complexity. The site needed to load fast on mobile.

## What I built

A static site with a product catalogue and a purchase flow that runs on the client's existing process, so there is no backend to host or pay for.

## Constraints

Client had a budget and no appetite for maintenance costs. No framework, no build step for them to manage. Had to load fast on a mobile connection since most of their customers browse on phones.
